Open Call · OFFGRID 2026

Trophy Design Competition

An open call to design the official OFFGRID Foundation Festival '26 trophy.

Participation

  • Open to students, Teaching Assistants and Faculty.
  • Entries may be submitted individually or as a group.
  • One entry per individual/group only.

Design Requirements

  • Material — Ceramic.
  • The trophy will be manufactured locally at the institution.
  • Design should consider ceramic fabrication, stability, scale, weight, durability and finish.
  • Three designs/trophies will be selected for First, Second, and Third Place.

Submission

  • Design drawings / images
  • Dimensions
  • Short concept statement
  • Material / fabrication information
  • Participant / group details

All entries must be submitted through the official OFFGRID submission portal.

Deadline: To Be Announced
