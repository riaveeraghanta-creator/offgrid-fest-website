Open Call · OFFGRID 2026

T-Shirt Design Competition

An open call to design the official OFFGRID Foundation Festival '26 T-shirt.

Participation

  • Open to students, Teaching Assistants and Faculty.
  • Entries may be submitted individually or as a group.
  • One entry per individual/group only.

Design Requirements

  • Artwork size: A4.
  • Multi-colour.
  • Placement: front, back, or both.
  • T-shirts should be kept in mind with the colours of black, grey & white.
  • Design should be suitable for practical production.
  • Use of AI will be disqualifying.

Submission Format

  • PNG
  • JPEG / JPG
  • Editable AI file
  • T-shirt mock-up
  • Participant / group details

Submission

All entries must be submitted through the official OFFGRID submission portal.

Deadline: To Be Announced
